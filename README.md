# Trino

**Pick 3 daily tasks. Build streaks. Stay consistent.**

## What is Trino?

Trino is a daily task app built around one simple rule: pick exactly 3 tasks each day and try to complete them. It's not about perfection — it's about showing up consistently. Complete at least 2 out of 3 and your streak stays alive.

## How it works

- **Pick your 3** — Choose three tasks to focus on each day
- **Build streaks** — Complete them consistently to keep your streak going
- **See your progress** — Watch your year fill up on a contribution-style grid
